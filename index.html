About

I am a graduate student at Carnegie Mellon University, where I design and build enterprise-scale data platforms that power analytics across a multi-entity FMCG manufacturing organization spanning Nigeria, Ghana, Egypt, South Africa, UAE, Singapore, and other countries.

My work centers on SAP S/4HANA as the system of record, Microsoft Fabric (Lakehouse, Warehouse, semantic models), Power BI, and a medallion architecture (Bronze → Silver → Gold) with OT/IoT pipelines. I hold the Microsoft Certified Fabric Data Engineer Associate (DP-700) and Fabric Analytics Engineer Associate (DP-600) certifications.

I am an active Microsoft Fabric community practitioner, speaker, and workshop facilitator. More broadly, I am passionate about building production-grade data platforms for manufacturing environments, developing robust data governance frameworks, and enabling data-driven decision-making at scale.
