About

I am a graduate student at Carnegie Mellon University exploring the intersection of machine learning, security, and privacy. My research spans federated learning, differential privacy, adversarial machine learning, and multimodal systems, with experience applying these methods to real-world problems in finance and healthcare.

I bring a combination of research, engineering, and industry experience, from developing ML systems to building data-driven solutions in production environments. I am interested in challenging problems where machine learning meets real-world constraints, and in building systems that are secure, reliable, and useful.
